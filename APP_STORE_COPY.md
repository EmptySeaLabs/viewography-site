# Viewography App Store presentation

Prepared for review. These changes have not been entered in App Store Connect.

Apple controls the App Store header layout and background. The developer controls the app name, subtitle, icon and marketing assets. A fuller page comes primarily from the screenshot sequence, promotional text and description.

## App name

Viewography: TV & Movies

Keep the existing name: it identifies the product and the category clearly.

## Recommended subtitle

Watch more. Spend less.

## Promotional text

Track what you love. Plan when to keep, pause or resume streaming. Free during our Founders’ Round. No accounts, ads, tracking, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Description

MORE GREAT WATCHING. LESS WASTED STREAMING.

Your movies. Your shows. A smarter plan for the services you pay for.

Viewography brings your viewing life together: what you’ve watched, what’s up next, and which streaming services are worth keeping right now.

FREE DURING OUR FOUNDERS’ ROUND

Every feature is free. No subscriptions. No ads. No in-app purchases.

A STREAMING PLAN BUILT AROUND YOU

Your watchlist and viewing progress tell part of the story. Release timing, subscriptions, costs and renewal dates tell the rest.

Viewography brings them together to recommend when to keep, pause or resume services. Finish a season before renewal. Save another show for later. Come back when there’s more you want to watch.

You make subscription changes with the provider. Viewography helps you decide what to change and when. Mark a canceled service as no longer auto-renewing, and see what to finish before access ends.

NO ACCOUNTS. NO ADS. NO TRACKING.

No separate account or signup. No trackers or third-party analytics. Your library lives on your iPhone, with a recovery copy in your own iCloud.

Sharing is optional. View Buddies uses Apple’s iCloud sharing, stays off until you turn it on, and lets you choose what to share.

YOUR NEXT WATCH, WITHOUT THE ENDLESS SCROLL

Home brings tonight’s pick, new episodes and what’s leaving your services together. Your Watchlist puts what’s ready on your services up next. Get suggestions based on your services and taste, or explore by service, genre and year.

YOUR SERVICES, EXACTLY

Tell Viewography how you get each service: its own app, an add-on channel through Prime Video or Apple TV, or your live TV lineup. Your plan reflects what you can actually watch.

FOR THE LOVE OF THE WATCH

• Track movies, episodes, whole seasons and rewatches
• Keep ratings and watch history
• See your viewing life through stats and challenges
• Share a watchlist with a View Buddy and send titles with a note
• Import history, ratings and watchlist from a public Trakt profile

Available for iPhone. Requires iOS 27.0 or later.

Made by Empty Sea Labs.
Questions or ideas: support@viewography.app

Movie and TV information from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability from JustWatch.

## Before updating the listing

- Confirm current App Store Connect field limits and which fields can be edited without a new app version.
- Verify the release’s supported operating system and regional availability.
- Check existing keyword metadata before changing it; it was not inspected for this draft.
- Keep “during our Founders’ Round” alongside the free claim. This copy does not promise permanent pricing.
- Apple’s header cannot be expanded into a custom website hero.
