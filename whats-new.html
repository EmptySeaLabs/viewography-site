Current Apple iOS beta

S4E1 · Ready Player One

Version 4.0 (35)

Available through TestFlight. Requires an iPhone running iOS 27.

Features

Up Next

The next episode of every show you're following and the movies you've saved, filtered to the services you pay for.

A Watchlist that sorts itself

What you can watch now, what's coming soon, what's in theaters, and what you can rent or buy.

What to Watch

Suggestions based on your services and taste, plus Explore for browsing movies and TV by where they stream, genre and year.

Streaming Plan

Its own tab. Add what each service costs and when it renews, and see which to keep and which to pause.

Your services, organized

Paid subscriptions, add-on channels, free services and TV access, so Viewography knows what you can actually watch.

Episode tracking

Mark episodes or whole seasons watched, set the date you watched them, start a show partway through, and undo anything.

Stats and challenges

Your viewing life in numbers, with milestones and challenges along the way.

View Buddies and Our List

Invite friends with a link or QR code, see what they're watching, and share a watchlist with one of them. You choose exactly what you share. Off unless you turn it on.

Bring your history from Trakt

Import your watch history, ratings and watchlist from a public Trakt profile in a few minutes. Nothing is sent back to Trakt.

Private by design

Your library lives on your iPhone with a backup in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Release notes

S4E1 · Ready Player One

4.0 (35) Apple iOS beta
  • Home comes first. What's up next, what's leaving and what's new, on one screen.
  • One Watchlist. Up Next and Watchlist are now one tab. Up next sits at the top.
  • Search with filters. In What to Watch, search opens Explore: pick a service, genre or year.
  • Your services, exactly. Tell us how you get a service, like AMC+ through Prime Video. Live TV counts too.
  • Services that end. Turn off Auto-renew and the Plan shows what to finish before it ends.
  • Share with any buddy. Send a title to any buddy, with or without an Our List.
Previous builds

S3E4 · You've Got Mail

3.0 (34) Apple iOS beta
  • Pick for tonight. Tap ✨ on Up Next, Watchlist or What to Watch. Choose what, how long and who, and get three picks.
  • Share a title. The share arrow on a title page sends a card with your rating and a link.
  • Share with a buddy. Send a title, with a note, to a buddy you have an Our List with.
  • A bell for your buddies. Invites, shared titles and thanks land in Activity, with a notification.
  • History by date. In History's filters, pick From and To. The list changes right away.
  • Buddies stay connected. After you remove a buddy or leave View Buddies, inviting again reconnects you.

S3E3 · Speed

3.0 (32) Apple iOS beta
  • More in one tap. On a title page, More opens one list, with each action once.
  • Straight to your episodes. Set your watch progress opens the episode list right away.
  • Rate in quarter stars. In Rate Your Library, pick how full the star is, then tap Save.
  • Fold and keep your place. On iPhone Duo, a title page stays open when you fold or unfold.
  • Also fixed: your iCloud backup copy keeps updating after moving between builds.

S3E2 · Catch Me If You Can

3.0 (31) Apple iOS beta
  • Canada, UK and Australia. Choose your region in Settings. Services, where to watch and prices follow it, in your currency.
  • One Watched button. One tap marks a movie watched, or a show Completed or Caught Up. Undo is right there.
  • Set your watch progress. Pick the last episode you watched, from a season grid and an episode list.
  • Your title page shows where you are: Watching · 1 of 8, Caught Up, Completed or Dropped. Dropped a show? Resume watching from its card.
  • Up Next. The watched circle sits next to the episode, off the poster.
  • New episodes sooner. Shows you're caught up on pick up new episodes within hours, not days.
  • View Buddies invites. Pasting or scanning an invite link works reliably.

S3E1 · Everything Everywhere All at Once

3.0 (30) Apple iOS beta
  • A plan for your services. Set what you want to spend each month. Plan works out what to pause and when to come back, and tells you what waits.
  • Your month at a glance. See when your shows air and when to make a change. Tap a day to open your service or mark it done.
  • Up Next as a TV guide. Switch Up Next to Guide to scroll your services like channels, with free channels at the bottom.
  • Watch first. Up Next and your Watchlist put first the shows on a service that's about to pause.
  • Also new: rewatch anything, tag the View Buddies you watched with, quarter stars, the Me tab, a History you can slice and dice, and Light mode.

S2E8 · Friends

2.0 (28) Apple iOS beta
  • View Buddies. Invite friends with a link or QR code, see what they're watching, and choose exactly what you share. Off unless you turn it on.
  • Our List. A watchlist you share with one buddy, right in your Watchlist.
  • Plan tab. Your Streaming Plan has its own tab: see which services to keep and which to pause.
  • The Me tab. Stats is now Me, with your Buddies and Settings.
  • Search people and genres. Find an actor or director, or type a genre like rom-com.
  • Smoother and safer. Smoother scrolling, What to Watch rows that refill as you watch, faster actor pages, and a way to replace your iCloud copy if backups pause after restoring a smaller backup.
  • Fixes for a crash while posters load, See More, and long-pressing shows that haven't aired.

S2E7 · Home Alone

2.0 (25) Apple iOS beta
  • What's New in the app. After an update, a short screen shows what changed.
  • Up Next your way. Choose whether TV or movies comes first.
  • Streaming Services. Every tab works like Paid. Price a live TV service, or remove one you dropped.
  • Change a watch date on episodes too, and the calendar moves between months.
  • Skipped seasons list their episodes, crossed out.
  • Bigger screens and landscape. Two-pane title pages, more posters in Explore and full backdrops.
  • Safer backups. Your iCloud recovery copy is protected while a new install is still downloading it, and "start from season 3" now survives backups.
  • Support Viewography. An optional tip jar in Settings. Tips are free while in TestFlight.
  • Fixes for Mark Watched from Explore, unwatching episodes on shows split into seasons, rating Undo, See More, search and large CSV imports.

S2E6 · Saturday Night Live

2.0 (24) Apple iOS beta
  • Live TV. Add YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, DirecTV Stream, Fubo, Sling or Philo, your cable or satellite provider, or an antenna. Viewography knows their channels and shows where to watch, like "YouTube TV · Food Network". Your streaming services always come first.
  • A simpler where-to-watch. The main answer first, then only the services you have, with everything else under "More ways to watch".
  • Rating takes a deliberate tap. Stars no longer change when you scroll past them. Tap Rate, pick your stars and save, with Undo.
  • Watchlist knows what's next. Shows you're caught up on sit second, sorted by the next episode: Today, Tomorrow, In 3 days, or Next season.
  • Up Next opens faster, with your movies there right away.
  • What to Watch fills in faster as you scroll, with the next rows loading before you reach them.
  • Poster captions name just the service, like "HBO Max".
  • Shows that are a season of another show on TMDB, like The Traitors: New Blood, can be matched to that season, keeping your history.
  • Fixes for posters flashing their title when tapped, the Done button covering rows, and logos.

S2E5 · Everything Everywhere All at Once

2.0 (23) Apple iOS beta
  • Watch Later. Keep a title on your Watchlist but out of Up Next, in its own Watch Later rows. Watching it, or removing Watch Later, brings it back.
  • Where each episode streams. When a show is split across services, Viewography shows where the episode you're up to can be watched, including new episodes on networks through TV Access.
  • Episode details. Tap an episode for its image, summary, cast and crew, and where to watch it. Tap anyone for their filmography. New episodes from the last 14 days are marked NEW.
  • A redesigned Explore. One row of filters for where it streams, genre and year, with results you can watch in the US.
  • Up Next fits on one screen on current iPhones.
  • After an import, Up Next fills in first, and a large library finishes in minutes instead of hours.
  • Long-running shows load every season, and you can mark a season unwatched.

S2E4 · Back to the Future

2.0 (22) Apple iOS beta
  • Trakt import is easier: find it anytime in Settings, paste your profile link or type your username (underscores work), and get clear help if your profile is private. The last step now says exactly what it will add.
  • While a big import fills in, Up Next and Watchlist show "Getting your library ready" with progress.
  • Settings is organized into simple panels.
  • Streaming services have a new Add-ons tab for channels you get through Prime Video, Apple TV or Roku. Network apps like Bravo, FX and TCM now sit under TV Access.
  • "Find more" on Up Next opens just that kind of title, with a button for all of What to Watch.
  • Long titles fit neatly on posters.
  • Watchlist shows "Checking availability…" while it checks, and movies in theaters move out of "Availability to check" more reliably.
  • Movies that aren't out yet no longer offer Watched At or Owned.
  • Viewography is now iPhone only.
