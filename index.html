BEFORE THE OPENING CREDITS

A few good
questions.

Is Viewography free?

Yes. Every feature is free during our Founders’ Round, with no subscriptions, no ads and no in-app purchases.

How does the Streaming Planner work?

It uses your watchlist, viewing progress, release timing and the services you have access to, alongside the costs and renewal dates you enter, to recommend when to keep, pause or resume subscriptions. It can suggest saving shows for later so a future subscription period has more you want to watch.

Does it cancel subscriptions for me?

No. You manage subscriptions with each provider. Viewography helps you decide what to change and when. When you mark a canceled service as no longer auto-renewing, it highlights what to finish before access ends.

Which devices does it support?

Viewography is available for iPhone and requires iOS 27.0 or later. Check the App Store listing for current compatibility and availability in your region.

Can I bring my history from Trakt?

Yes. Import your watch history, ratings and watchlist from a public Trakt profile. Viewography does not send changes back to Trakt.

Do I have to share anything?

No. View Buddies is optional and off until you enable it. You choose what to share. Your personal library has a recovery copy in your own iCloud.
